04 / Questions

What I want to understand

Technology is the instrument, not the question. I am interested in what happens organisationally before, during, and after the software itself works as designed.

  • 01: VALUEWhy comparable construction technologies produce successful, partial, or failed outcomes across firms.
  • 02: GOVERNANCEHow corporate standardisation can coexist with project-level autonomy in digital implementation.
  • 03: FINANCEHow firms connect technical capability with expected operational and financial value.
  • 04: METHODHow structured classification and comparative case analysis can make organisational evidence legible.
