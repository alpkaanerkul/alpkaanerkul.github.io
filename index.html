Focus

What I'm trying to understand

Technology is the instrument, not the question. The question is what happens organisationally around it; before, during, and after the software itself works as designed.

  • 01 — VALUEBusiness-value realisation from construction software: why comparable tools produce successful, partial, or failed outcomes across firms.
  • 02 — GOVERNANCEThe balance between corporate standardisation and project-level autonomy in digital implementation.
  • 03 — FINANCEHow firms evaluate investments, allocate resources, manage commercial exposure, and connect technical capability with expected operational and financial value.
  • 04 — METHODStructured classification and comparative case analysis as ways of making messy organisational evidence legible.
